## The shape of it

Not one project: a decade of shipping and maintaining real client work, consolidated.
The point of this card is breadth and reliability, the unglamorous senior signal that
a lot of things went live and stayed live.

## Agency: 40AU

At 40AU I maintained the brand sites for four concepts of 4Top Hospitality, an
employee-owned restaurant group across the Southeast: etc., etch, Amerigo, and Char
in Nashville. All four are still live, largely as I set them up, and years in
production without drama is its own quiet endorsement. Alongside them sat work that
stays credit-only: the home site for Vanderbilt's "My Day" early-diabetes tracking
app, an embedded stint on the NARUS "Compassion" nursing dashboard, brand work
through Fullscreen Direct (Megadeth, Eric Church, Kid Rock, Linkin Park), and bank
web forms and stored procedures. Healthcare and bank internals are confidential, so
those are described, precisely, and not shown.

## Platform: Horton Group

With Horton Group's senior developer I built a reusable templated application on
Angular and KeystoneJS, designed so the team could re-skin it for future clients
instead of rebuilding each time. The interesting decisions were the seams: what to
make configurable, what to keep fixed, how one codebase serves many client skins
without forking. A second win there was remediation, pulling a bloated WordPress
site (Sisters on the Fly) back to something maintainable. Architecting for reuse and
rescuing drifted systems are both senior work; client assets are not showable, so
this thread will be told with an abstracted "one template, many skins" diagram.

## Independent

Freelance builds I designed, built, onboarded, and maintained: TNPRCV, Rebuilding
Together Nashville, Foundation Christian Academy, and Sombra Mexican Kitchen. Small
engagements, full ownership, still standing.
