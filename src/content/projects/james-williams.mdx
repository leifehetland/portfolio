## The brief

James Williams, LLC is a family-owned Metro Atlanta general contractor with thirty-plus
years of work behind it. Our goal was to come up with a site that matched the quality of
work that Jim and his team provides.

## The after

The redesign is a full marketing site. A hero with clear calls to action, then a
credibility band up front: thirty-plus years, family-owned, service area. A services
grid opens into a fuller services section, and a draw-schedule explainer walks clients
through how construction financing actually pays out, which most contractor sites leave
out. Testimonials, the founder's story, qualifications, and a portfolio grid close it
out.
