## The brief

James Williams, LLC is a family-owned general contractor in Metro Atlanta with more
than thirty years of built work behind it, and a website that said almost none of
that: a centered logo, a one-line tagline, three photos, and a row of social icons.
The job was to make the site carry the weight the business already had earned.

## The after

The redesign is a full editorial marketing site. A hero with clear calls to action,
then a credibility band up front: thirty-plus years, family-owned, the service area.
A "what we build" services grid leads into a comprehensive services section, and a
draw-schedule explainer walks clients through how construction financing actually
pays out, which is the question every client has and almost no contractor answers on
their site. Testimonials, the founder's story, qualifications, a service-area block,
and a portfolio grid round it out, closing on a strong final call to action.
