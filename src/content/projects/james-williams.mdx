## The brief

James Williams, LLC is a family-owned Metro Atlanta general contractor with thirty-plus
years of work behind it and a site that showed almost none of it: a logo, one tagline,
three photos, and some social icons. My job was to make the site carry the weight the
business had already earned.

## The after

I rebuilt it as a full marketing site. A hero with clear calls to action, a credibility
band up front (thirty-plus years, family-owned, service area), a services grid into a
fuller services section, and a draw-schedule explainer that walks clients through how
construction financing actually pays out, the question every client has and almost no
contractor answers. Testimonials, the founder's story, qualifications, and a portfolio
grid close it out.
