## The throughline

Before the web there were games, and around the engineering there has always been film
and music. One practice, not a pile of hobbies: Leif writes, shoots, edits, and scores
the same way he builds software, with the same instinct for structure and pacing.

## Film

Two shorts of his own: "The Traveller" (co-writer, cinematographer, composer, editor)
and "Lights Among Us" (co-writer, 1st AD and set support, editor). Around them, on-set
craft on other people's projects: 1st AD, PA, boom operator, camera operator, and grip,
including the feature "Content" (grip, camera and electrical, 2019). Atlanta Film Society
PA Academy trained, 2025.

## Games

Punchy Fox was the studio attempt, built with his longtime collaborator Tyler Aldridge,
and it reached a playable prototype. The jams around it taught scope and finish: "Is
Anyone Out There?" did well at its jam, and "La Bamba," a Bomberman-style clone, got to a
playable build. Shipping something playable on a deadline carried into everything since.

## Score

Original score and theme music for the "Holed Up" podcast series, fifty episodes across
2024 and 2025, plus scores for his own films. Short excerpts will play inline once the
samples are cut and cleared.

## Why it belongs here

Most engineering portfolios stop at the code. This is here because the making, film,
games, music, is where the same instincts started. It is also the front door to the lab,
where the fuller body of experimental music, digital art, and film work will live. Clips,
game footage, and score samples land here once collaborator sign-offs are in.
