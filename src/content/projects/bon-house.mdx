## The brief

Bon House is a DIY music venue in Decatur: a 150-cap room with a PBR sponsorship and a
growing crowd. It needed a site with the energy of the room, not another quiet minimal
template.

## The build

I built it on a collage aesthetic that matches the space: brush display type, a
torn-paper photo hero, and one grid that holds the whole operation, the bands, the
venue, the studio, and the label. It runs on TypeScript and Next.js with a small design
system, so the team can add shows and sections themselves, without the layout drifting
or a developer in the loop.

## Why it belongs here

Next to the contractor and video-store work, Bon House shows range: a completely
different look, the same system discipline underneath. It is a client engagement, so I
show the site as built and keep individual band photos to where the agreements allow.
